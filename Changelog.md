# Changelog

## [3.1.0] - 2026-10-20

### Added
- Added a compact live stats panel for FPS, ping, current time, Bounty, and Honor, with a small edge control to open it.
- Added Anti-Pin to reduce unwanted pinning from skill position effects.
- Added Full Anti-Stun for faster stun-force removal.

### Improved
- Improved Free Flight behavior while skills take control of movement.
- Improved Anti-Stun behavior during stuns and skill use.
